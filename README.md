A [make.fun](https://make.fun) entry for **The Excuse That Almost Works** ($2): write the one line a gamer texts their boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week — at most 20 words, reading like a hurried SMS, built on one boring, specific, almost-believable detail.

## The entry

> Gas safety check, engineer booked 8-1, can't leave flat unattended or they cancel and rebook in 3 weeks.

18 words, 104 characters, one line. The humour is in the excuse's own logic: nobody leaves the flat during a gas safety check, because the penalty for missing the window is a three-week rebooking — the kind of dull bureaucratic dread a tired manager recognises instantly.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else: no network, no randomness, no arguments. Two runs produce byte-identical output.

```bash
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
